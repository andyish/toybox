# Amelia's ToyBox: Help & support

## Getting help

Found a problem, or have a question or an idea? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/andyish/toybox/issues) (a free GitHub account is needed) and we'll get back to you.

## How do I open Settings?

Press and hold the gear in the top corner of the home screen for about a second and a half, until the ring around it fills. A quick tap does nothing, so little fingers don't end up there by accident.

## How do I turn the sounds off?

Open Settings and switch off Sound effects.

## How do I change the look?

Open Settings and pick a different picture under Theme. The app asks once, the first time it opens.

## How do I clear the colouring?

To clear one picture, press and hold the eraser button at the top of that picture (Undo brings the colours back). To clear every picture, open Settings and choose Reset coloured pictures.

## What do the two colouring modes do?

Choose colours: pick a colour, then tap to paint with it. Tap to colour: every tap fills a part of the picture with its right colour, which is easier for the youngest children.

## Does the app need the internet?

No. Every game works offline.

## Does the app collect any data?

No. Nothing leaves the device. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
