Privacy policy

Amelia's ToyBox

The short version

Amelia's ToyBox doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no in-app purchases and no links out of the app.

Nothing leaves this device

The app has no analytics, tracking or third-party services, and it never sends anything over the internet. Every game works offline.

What the app saves

So things are just as you left them, the app saves the colouring pictures, the dress-up outfit, the aeroplane you built, the chosen look, the colouring mode and the sound setting on this device only. You can clear the colouring with "Reset coloured pictures" in Settings, and deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Permissions

The app doesn't use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

The app is made for young children. Because it collects no information from anyone, there is nothing for us to see, share or delete.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this policy and the app's App Store privacy details before that version is released.

Questions

Use the support contact on the app's App Store page.

You can also reach us through the support page.
